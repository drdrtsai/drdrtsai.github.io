## Bio

I am a psychiatrist at the Massachusetts General Hospital and Professor of Psychiatry at Harvard Medical School. My research focuses on understanding how large-scale social pathogens such as stigma, discrimination, and structural violence affect the distribution of mental health outcomes both in the U.S. and in eastern/southern Africa. I have a secondary appointment as Associate Professor in the [Department of Epidemiology](https://hsph.harvard.edu/department/epidemiology/) at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, where I teach psychiatric epidemiology and co-direct the [T32 training program in psychiatric epidemiology and biostatistics](https://hsph.harvard.edu/department/epidemiology/t32-funding-opportunities/). In recognition of my sustained contributions to science I was elected to membership in the [American Society for Clinical Investigation](https://beta.the-asci.org) in 2021 and the [American College of Psychiatrists](https://www.acpsych.org) in 2023. Prior to my appointment at Mass General, I completed a postdoctoral research fellowship in the [Robert Wood Johnson Health and Society Scholars Program](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30809585/) at Harvard University; my residency training in general adult psychiatry at the [University of California at San Francisco](https://psychiatry.ucsf.edu/rtp); and my internship in medicine, neurology, and psychiatry at the [University of Washington](https://residency.psychiatry.uw.edu).
