Bio

I am a psychiatrist at the Massachusetts General Hospital and Professor of Psychiatry at Harvard Medical School. My research focuses on understanding how large-scale social pathogens such as stigma, discrimination, and structural violence affect the distribution of mental health outcomes both in the U.S. and in eastern/southern Africa. I have a secondary appointment as Associate Professor in the Department of Epidemiology at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, where I teach psychiatric epidemiology and co-direct the T32 training program in psychiatric epidemiology and biostatistics. In recognition of my sustained contributions to science I was elected to membership in the American Society for Clinical Investigation in 2021 and the American College of Psychiatrists in 2023. Prior to my appointment at Mass General, I completed a postdoctoral research fellowship in the Robert Wood Johnson Health and Society Scholars Program at Harvard University; my residency training in general adult psychiatry at the University of California at San Francisco; and my internship in medicine, neurology, and psychiatry at the University of Washington.
